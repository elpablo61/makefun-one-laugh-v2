## How it was built

Calibrated against 90 verified top-10 winning one-liners
(`judge-calibration-one-liner.md` §2d): one specific absurd image with a
named person and concrete numbers, a present-tense confession, and a
factual ending — no setup-punchline structure, no explanation of the joke.
8 variants were drafted across different styles, filtered on three points
(named specific detail / no explaining the punchline / one concept only),
then judged pairwise against the calibration winners. This line won every
pairing: it mirrors the toddler-transgression winner pattern (child does
something transgressive, adult deadpan) with two concrete prices and a
two-beat factual close.
